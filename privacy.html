Co-parenting Evidence Journal Privacy

Co-parenting Evidence Journal does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the current release.

Local Data

Visitation details, expense rows, notes, attachment labels, local drafts, and export packets stay on device unless the user chooses a system share or Files export destination.

Network And Purchases

The app does not require an account, server, court portal, attorney portal, co-parenting platform login, two-party messaging, payment transfer, location proof, or surveillance workflow.

System in-app purchase handles the lifetime unlock. Co-parenting Evidence Journal does not receive payment card details.

Record Organization Boundary

The app does not provide legal advice, create certified records, authenticate evidence, or guarantee admissibility or outcomes.
